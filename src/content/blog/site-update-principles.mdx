The operating rules for this site are intentionally simple:

- Do not distort the story just to chase the trend.
- Do not hide weak thinking behind jargon.
- Do not destroy the archive structure for a short-term traffic spike.

Consistency matters more than intensity. A site that can publish steadily every week is worth far more than a site that dumps ten posts in one burst and then disappears next month.
