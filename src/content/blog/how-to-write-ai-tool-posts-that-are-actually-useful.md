The biggest problem with AI tool content is simple: too many articles just paraphrase the product website.

What readers actually want to know is this:

- Who saves time with this tool?
- How fast can someone become productive with it?
- Is it worth paying for?
- Are there cheaper or sharper alternatives?

That is why AI tool writing works better when every post follows a decision-oriented structure:

1. Who it is for
2. What problem it solves
3. What the real hands-on experience feels like
4. Whether it deserves a permanent place in the workflow

If every review follows this structure, the archive becomes much more useful than a typical feature summary.
