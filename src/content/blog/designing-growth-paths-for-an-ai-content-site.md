A lot of sites focus only on shipping posts. After a few articles, the owners realize nobody is reading.

For an independent media site, categories matter, but growth paths matter more. Otherwise you are only placing content on the internet instead of creating a traffic machine.

For a site like AInothing.top, three recurring growth motions make the most sense:

- Use AI signals posts to capture timely attention
- Use revenue breakdowns to earn shares and saves
- Use tool posts and tutorials to win search traffic

That mix gives you a short-term traffic engine, an evergreen search layer, and a stronger chance of turning the site into a long-term asset.
