# Revenue breakdown article template

Use this template for AInothing.top posts in the Revenue Breakdowns category.

Goal: turn a flashy money claim into a structured, useful teardown.

## Frontmatter template

```md

  - [Monetization]

```

## Editorial checklist

Before publishing, answer these clearly:

- How much money is being claimed?
- What does the number actually represent: revenue, profit, MRR, ARR, cash collected, GMV, or run rate?
- What is being sold?
- Where does demand come from?
- What is the conversion path?
- What costs, churn, refunds, or labor do readers need to know?
- Which parts are repeatable and which parts are founder-specific?
- What can the reader apply immediately?

## Recommended structure

### Opening hook

State the money claim, then immediately explain why the raw number alone is not enough.

Example:

> A founder says this niche site makes $12,000 per month. Interesting — but the useful question is whether that number comes from durable search traffic, a temporary ranking spike, or a monetization mix that only works in this exact niche.

### 1. The claim and what it actually measures

- State the number exactly.
- Define the metric precisely.
- Note the time period.
- Mention how reliable the source is.

Suggested prompt:

- Is this monthly revenue, annualized run rate, profit, or gross sales?
- Is it a screenshot, interview quote, public filing, or founder post?

### 2. What was sold

Explain the offer clearly.

Suggested prompt:

- SaaS subscription?
- Newsletter sponsorships?
- Agency service?
- Affiliate content site?
- Digital product or template pack?

### 3. Where traffic or demand came from

Map the acquisition engine.

Suggested prompt:

- SEO
- X / LinkedIn / YouTube / TikTok
- Existing audience
- Partnerships
- Paid ads
- Community distribution
- Outbound sales

### 4. How conversion worked

Describe the funnel.

Suggested prompt:

- Landing page
- Email capture
- Demo call
- Free trial
- Checkout flow
- Pricing and packaging
- Case studies / social proof

### 5. What costs or hidden constraints matter

Force the post past top-line revenue.

Suggested prompt:

- Ad spend
- Team or contractor costs
- Churn
- Refunds
- Time intensity
- Dependence on one platform or one customer segment

### 6. Is the model repeatable?

Give an honest replication verdict.

Use a simple rating if useful:

- High repeatability
- Medium repeatability
- Low repeatability

Then explain the real bottleneck:

- founder skill
- timing
- brand
- network
- capital
- technical edge
- traffic moat

### 7. What readers should steal from the case

End with concrete lessons.

Suggested prompt:

- What is the pattern here?
- Which part of the system matters most?
- What should a solo operator test first?

## Fast headline formulas

- Breaking down a $[number]/month [business type] beyond the screenshot
- What a $[number]/month [business model] really depends on
- How to read a [business type] revenue claim without getting fooled
- Inside a [traffic source]-driven [business type] making $[number]/month

## AInothing.top standard

A good revenue post should help readers answer:

1. Is the number real enough to care about?
2. Where did the money actually come from?
3. Can I learn a reusable pattern from it?
